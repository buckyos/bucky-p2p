# Harness And Process Trigger

## Required Coverage
- Design states the task-routing, checking, evidence, CI, template, or acceptance behavior being changed.
- Validation includes a generated-scaffold or checker scenario that fails before the change when practical and passes afterward.

## Reviewer Focus
- Check template/rule contradictions, missing generated paths, index coverage, bypassable wording, inactive rules entering context, and checks that pass without validating their intended condition.
